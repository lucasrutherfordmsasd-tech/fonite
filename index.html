Relic Rush 100

100 players ride a flying bus over a hilly island of towns, and everyone has to jump. You land with nothing, so open chests for weapons, ammo, shield and healing items. Weapons come in six rarities, from Common up to Mythic, and the four bosses drop the best ones. Chop trees for wood and build walls, floors and ramps. Eliminate all 99 players to win.

Mouse: full 360° turn · WASD move · Shift sprint · SPACE jump / glider · Click shoot · Right-click aim
E open chest · R reload · 1-3 or wheel: weapon · Z bandage · X medkit · C small shield · V big shield
Build: hold F to chop trees · Q build mode · 1 wall, 2 floor, 3 ramp · click to place (10 wood)
